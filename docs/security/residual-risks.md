# Residual risks and known limitations

Plain-language list of what Nulavio does **not** protect against today. It is linked from the start page and shown in plain language in the README.

## Out of scope by nature

1. **Malware already running as your user.** An infostealer that runs with your account can read your Nulavio profile (cookies, sessions, history), read engine-saved passwords if no Primary Password is set (or capture it when typed), add extensions, change settings and read memory of your processes on many systems. Profile permissions (0700/0600) protect against *other* accounts, not against your own. *Nulavio Guard* only detects cheap, accidental or legacy weakening; malware can bypass it.
2. **Malware with root access.** Everything is exposed.
3. **Physical access to an unlocked session.**

## Google services you sign in to

Nulavio stays compatible with Google's services: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Meet, YouTube, and "Sign in with Google" on other sites (decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-13, #220). When you sign in to one of them, you have accepted Google's terms for that service. Nulavio does not try to hide you from it. While you use it, Google still sees:

- what you do in that service, the account you are signed in with, and your internet address;
- the device details any site can read: the window size, the language, the time zone, and what is listed under "What a page reads" in the [fingerprinting study](../research/fingerprinting.md#what-a-page-reads).

What Nulavio does is keep that inside the service, except what you hand over yourself: "Sign in with Google" on another site tells Google that your account signed in there (R73). Total Cookie Protection keeps Google's cookies to Google's own sites. uBlock Origin's default lists and Strict tracking protection list Google's analytics and ad scripts, so they are blocked on other sites; a test that pins this on local pages is still to come (#220). The engine's clean-link lists include Google's tracking parameters (`gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid` for any site, `si` for YouTube's addresses); #158 adds more, `dclid` among them, and no test on a Nulavio build pins the Google ones yet. Three things reduce what Google learns further, and each is your choice:

1. **A separate profile for Google.** Its cookies and sign-ins then live in that profile only, and routing rules can open Google's links there ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-nulavio-profiles-and-routing.md)). "Sign in with Google" on another site then needs a Google sign-in in that site's profile too. The rules offered when you add a Google account are not built yet (#220).
2. **A different network route for that profile** (a VPN), so Google does not see your home address (#217, not built yet).
3. **Another service** where one does the job: another mail provider, calendar or search engine. When Nulavio offers a choice of service, Google's is one of them, in a neutral order and never preselected. Of those choices, only the search engine's exists today, and it does not list Google yet (#151); mail and calendar links are #218.
