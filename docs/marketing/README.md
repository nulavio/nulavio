## The eight rules

They were approved as written on 2026-11-14 and are in [#176](https://github.com/nulavio/nulavio/issues/176#issue-body). In short:

1. The product is always real: the interface is recorded from a real build by a script, never generated.
2. Every claim links to its evidence. Planned features are marked as planned. No absolute claims, and no comparison without a dated source.
3. No fake people, no invented quotes or numbers, no imitation of a real person's voice.
4. AI is disclosed in every video and with each platform's label.
5. Every generated asset has its tool, plan, date and terms in a manifest. Tools whose terms forbid commercial use, or that keep rights over the output, are not used.
6. No third-party service is shown as part of Nulavio or as the recommended choice. Mozilla and Firefox are named only as the [strategy](../brand/strategy.md#correct-use-of-the-name-and-marks) allows.
7. Recordings use only test profiles, fixture sites and test accounts. Every frame is checked before publishing.
8. Publishing, replies, contact with press or communities, and spending money belong to the maintainer.
