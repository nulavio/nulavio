Waitlist

Leave your email and we will tell you when Nulavio reaches your system. Fedora first, Ubuntu next.

What we keep

  • the email address you typed;
  • the language of the page you signed up on;
  • the system, if you pick one;
  • the date and time you signed up, to keep the order of arrival.

We do not keep your IP address or anything about your browser with the sign-up. The list lives in a database at Cloudflare, the company that hosts this site and that receives every request as any web server does. Your address is used for one message: the one saying Nulavio is available for you. No newsletter, and it is never passed on.

Leave the list

Type the address you signed up with. It is deleted on the spot, no questions asked.
