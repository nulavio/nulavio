Waitlist

Leave your email and we will tell you when Nulavio reaches your system. Fedora first, Ubuntu next.

What we keep

  • the email address you typed;
  • the language of the page you signed up on;
  • the system, if you pick one;
  • the date and time you signed up, to keep the order of arrival;
  • a fingerprint (SHA-256) of your private link, to recognise it when you leave. The link itself is not kept.

We do not keep your IP address or anything about your browser with the sign-up. The list lives in a database at Cloudflare, the company that hosts this site and that receives every request as any web server does. Your address is used for one message: the one saying Nulavio is available for you. No newsletter, and it is never passed on.

Leave the list

When you sign up you get a private link: the address of the page that comes right after the form. Open that link and use the “Delete my email” button. The address is deleted on the spot, no questions asked.

We ask for the link because knowing an address does not prove the sign-up is yours: without it, anyone could take you off the list. Signing up the same address again changes nothing about the first sign-up and gives no new link that works. So if someone else signed your address up before you did, the link that works is theirs: write to us.

Lost the link, signed up before it existed, or someone else signed up your address? Write to security@nulavio.net from the address on the list. We answer to that address and delete it once you confirm.
