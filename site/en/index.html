Protected before the first click

No hardening guide and no extensions to hunt down. Nulavio opens with uBlock Origin running, tracking protection in Strict mode and HTTPS-Only in every window. The engine's phishing and malware protection stays on.

  • Ads and trackers blocked from the first launch
  • Every default says why it is there and what it costs
  • Everything can be undone, per site or all at once

No fine print: blocking trackers breaks a site now and then. Two clicks turn protection off for that site only.

Work, banking and personal life, each in its own profile

Each profile has its own logins, cookies, history and extensions. You say once where each site opens, and Nulavio takes every link to the right profile: the ones that come from other apps, the ones you type and the ones you click on pages.

  • Switch profiles in one click, with colour and name always in view
  • Temporary profile: gone from the disk when you close its window
  • A template for sensitive sites: cleared on close, no installs from web pages

No fine print: profiles keep your data apart. They do not hide your IP address.

Profiles encrypted on disk. You hold the key.

A protected profile stays encrypted while it is locked: whoever copies the files, or takes the laptop, reads nothing. To open it, use the profile's password, your system login, a FIDO2 security key or your phone's biometrics, with Nulavio Companion.

  • The method you choose really releases the key: an approval alone opens nothing
  • Locks again when you close the profile's last window
  • A recovery code is created along with the profile

No fine print: there is no Nulavio master key. Lose every method and the recovery code, and the profile is gone.

If something changes from outside, you hear about it

Data-stealing programs work while the browser is closed: they install an extension, add a certificate, flip a setting. On the next launch, Guard shows each change and you decide whether to undo it or keep it.

  • Watches extensions, protective settings, certificates, policies, search engines and the user.js file
  • Refuses to open a profile whose engine defences were switched off

No fine print: Guard tells you; it does not prevent. No browser protects against a malicious program already running on your computer.

AI that goes only as far as you let it

Connect Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client. Each agent works in an isolated profile, away from your logins, at the autonomy level you choose. The built-in assistant uses the AI service you prefer, even a model running on your own computer, and nothing leaves before you click Send.

  • Four levels: Restricted, Assisted, Autonomous and Full control
  • Permissions per agent, per site and per duration, revocable on the spot
  • Emergency stop: one click and every agent stops

No fine print: in Full control, the agent does what you would do. That is why this level asks for a separate confirmation.

Also built in

What you would assemble by hand in another browser is already done, and every choice can be undone.

  • Your passwords stay where they are

    Proton Pass, KeePassXC or Bitwarden ready in one step, through each one's official extension. Nulavio does not build a second vault.

  • What you type goes nowhere without asking

    Search suggestions stay off until you allow them, one search engine at a time. DuckDuckGo by default; Brave Search, Mojeek, Kagi or your own SearXNG instance whenever you like.

  • You choose the DNS

    Your computer's resolver or encrypted DNS with Cloudflare, Quad9, Mullvad or your own. The question comes up once, on the first tab.

  • Downloads without surprises

    A downloaded program never opens straight from the browser. You get the warning and the folder, not a running executable.

  • Made for Linux

    Wayland, KDE and GNOME, system portals, KDE Connect and KRunner. Signed packages for Fedora and Ubuntu, updated by your package manager.

  • Open and verifiable

    Code under MPL-2.0. The engine is built from the source Mozilla signs, with {{patches}} documented patches, and every package ships with its list of components (SBOM).

Frequently asked questions

When will I be able to use Nulavio?

On Fedora, now: the first alpha is out through the signed dnf repository (download). Ubuntu comes next. Join the waitlist and you get an email when it is your system's turn.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Nulavio is free and open source, under the MPL-2.0 licence. It has no ads and no account, and it sells no data, because it collects none.

Is it a modified Firefox?

Nulavio uses Gecko, Mozilla's engine, built from the official Firefox {{firefox}} source with {{patches}} small, documented patches. Site isolation, certificate checks and security fixes are the engine's own. The window, the profiles, Guard and the rest are Nulavio's. Nulavio is not affiliated with Mozilla.

Do my extensions work?

Yes. Extensions from addons.mozilla.org install as in any Gecko browser, and uBlock Origin comes installed.

What about Windows, macOS or my phone?

Linux first. When you join the waitlist you can say which system you use, and those answers decide the order. On Android, Nulavio Companion unlocks your profiles; it is not a browser.

Does Nulavio make me anonymous?

No. It reduces tracking, but sites and your internet provider still see your IP address. If you need anonymity, use Tor Browser.

What do you do with my email?

We keep the address, the language, the system you pick and the date. We use it for one message: the one saying Nulavio is available for you. No newsletter, and it is never passed on. To leave, use the private link you get when you sign up (how to leave).
