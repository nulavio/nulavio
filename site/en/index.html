The web is yours. So is your data.

Nulavio is an open-source browser for Linux, built on Mozilla's Gecko engine. It comes private by default, blocks ads and trackers, and works with your password manager.

Private by default

Strict tracking protection, HTTPS-Only and the Global Privacy Control signal are on from the start. Nothing is sent to Nulavio: the project has no servers that receive your browsing data.

Ad blocking

uBlock Origin is included, at a pinned version checked at install time. If a site breaks, turn it off for that site only.

Your passwords, in your manager

Proton Pass and KeePassXC through their official extensions; Bitwarden in testing. Nulavio does not read your vault.

Engine protections kept

Sandboxing, site isolation, certificate checks and phishing and malware protection stay on. Downloaded installers and scripts show a warning before you open them.

What a browser cannot fix

No browser protects against malware already running on your computer. We document the known limits and will publish them with the code.
